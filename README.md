# news-image-01 — The World Today: Friday, 13 November 2026

Entry for the make.fun competition **news-image-01** — "Sum up 13 November's world news in one image" ($250, top 100 entries share the prize).

## The image

A newspaper front page, **"THE WORLD TODAY — FRIDAY, 13 NOVEMBER 2026"**, summing up the day's four biggest world stories:

1. **NVIDIA CHIEF RESIGNS** — Jensen Huang steps down; shares plunge 16%. The day's biggest tech/business story: the Nvidia chief executive resigned amid reports of an affair with a woman alleged to be a Chinese spy, was summoned to testify before Congress, and the CFO became interim CEO.
2. **EUROPE JOINS UKRAINE PEACE TALKS** — US envoys Witkoff and Kushner met Putin in Moscow, then Zelensky in Dublin; Europe wins a seat at the next round of negotiations, and the largest prisoner exchange of the war is ahead.
3. **MICROSOFT 365 DOWN WORLDWIDE** — Outlook and Teams offline for a second day in a global outage affecting businesses, hospitals and police.
4. **TANKERS STRUCK NEAR HORMUZ** — Iran tensions rise as tankers are hit near the Strait of Hormuz and the US says no deal with Iran is needed.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) deterministically copies the bundled front-page image (`image.jpg`, 1024×1024 JPEG, 237 KB) to `/output/entry.png`. No network, no randomness, no clock — running it twice writes the exact same bytes.

## Sources

Headlines verified against multiple news briefings for Friday 13 November 2026 (Seven Lights Daily Brief, Open Chronicle Global News Brief, The Silk Paradox, Bangladesh Chronicle, Michael Brunger Daily Brief, 0400 Report).
